# Privacy Policy for LifeMeter

**Effective Date**: July 18, 2025  
**Last Updated**: July 18, 2025

## Introduction

LifeMeter ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application LifeMeter (the "App"). Our fundamental principle is simple: **we collect no personal data whatsoever**.

## Our Privacy-First Commitment

LifeMeter is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe that your financial information and usage patterns are private and should remain under your complete control. This policy outlines our commitment to maintaining your privacy while providing you with a valuable service.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

### Personal Information
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including but not limited to:
- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data or geographic information
- Biometric data or device fingerprints
- Social media profiles or account information

### Financial Information
We do not collect or transmit any financial data, including:
- Wage information or salary details
- Bank account or payment information

- Investment or financial portfolio information

### Usage Analytics
We do not collect any analytics or usage data, including:
- App usage patterns or frequency

### Device Information
We do not collect device-specific information, including:
- Device model, operating system version, or hardware specifications
- Network information or IP addresses
- Installed apps or device configuration
- Battery usage or performance data
- Storage usage or available space

## How Your Data Stays Private

### Local Storage Only
All data generated by LifeMeter remains exclusively on your device:

**Wage Information**: Your hourly wage is stored securely in the iOS Keychain using the `.accessibleAfterFirstUnlock` protection class. This ensures that your wage data is encrypted and only accessible after you unlock your device for the first time after a restart.

**Calculation History**: Your price calculations and work time conversions are stored locally using CoreData. This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable iCloud sync.

**App Settings**: Your preferences, currency selections, and app configurations are stored in iOS UserDefaults and remain local to your device.

**OCR Images**: When you scan receipts or photos, the images are processed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No images are uploaded to external servers or cloud services.

### Optional iCloud Sync

LifeMeter offers optional iCloud synchronization for your convenience:

**User Control**: iCloud sync is entirely optional and disabled by default. You must explicitly enable it in the app settings.

**Apple's Privacy**: When enabled, your data is synchronized through Apple's iCloud service, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

**Encryption**: All data synchronized through iCloud is encrypted both in transit and at rest using Apple's security protocols.

**Data Types**: Only your calculation history and app settings are synchronized. Your wage information remains in the local Keychain and is not synchronized.

### No Network Communication

LifeMeter operates entirely offline:

**No Servers**: We do not operate any servers or cloud infrastructure that could collect your data.

**No APIs**: The app does not communicate with any external APIs or web services.

**No Third-Party Services**: We do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

**No Tracking**: There are no tracking pixels, beacons, or other monitoring technologies in the app.

## Data Processing

### On-Device Processing
All data processing occurs locally on your device:

**Price Conversion**: Wage calculations and time conversions are performed using local algorithms without any external communication.

**OCR Text Recognition**: Receipt scanning and text recognition use Apple's on-device Vision framework. No images or text are sent to external services.

**Currency Formatting**: Currency conversion and formatting use iOS's built-in localization features without external exchange rate services.

**Animation Rendering**: Cat animations and UI rendering are handled entirely by your device's graphics processing unit.

### No Profiling or Targeting
We do not create user profiles or engage in any form of behavioral targeting:

**No User Profiles**: We do not build profiles based on your usage patterns or preferences.

**No Behavioral Analysis**: We do not analyze your behavior or create usage models.

**No Predictive Analytics**: We do not use machine learning or AI to predict your behavior or preferences.

**No Cross-App Tracking**: We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

## Third-Party Services

### Apple Services

**iOS Frameworks**: We use standard iOS frameworks (UIKit, SwiftUI, CoreData, Vision, WidgetKit) that operate locally on your device.

**App Store**: App distribution and updates are handled through Apple's App Store, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

**iCloud (Optional)**: If you enable iCloud sync, your data is handled according to Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy.

### No Other Third Parties
We do not integrate with any other third-party services, including:
- Analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- Advertising networks or ad servers
- Social media platforms or sharing services
- Cloud storage providers other than iCloud
- Payment processors or financial services
- Customer support or helpdesk platforms

## Security Measures

### Device-Level Security
Your data security relies on iOS's built-in security features:

**Keychain Encryption**: Wage data is protected by iOS Keychain encryption.

**App Sandbox**: The app operates within iOS's security sandbox, preventing unauthorized access to your data.

**Code Signing**: The app is digitally signed by Apple, ensuring its authenticity and integrity.

**Regular Updates**: We provide regular security updates through the App Store.

### No Network Security Needed
Since LifeMeter does not communicate over the network:

**No Data Transmission**: There is no risk of data interception during transmission.

**No Server Vulnerabilities**: We have no servers that could be compromised.

**No Man-in-the-Middle Attacks**: Network-based attacks are not possible.
